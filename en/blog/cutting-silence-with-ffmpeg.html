Cutting the silent parts out of a video with ffmpeg

In talking-head videos, the slowest job is cutting out every pause by hand. I automated it with ffmpeg's silencedetect filter and a short Python script.

Category:
VideographyMultimedia
Tags:
ffmpegsilencedetectPythonvideo editingReels

1. Find the silent parts

ffmpeg's silencedetect filter does not change the video. It only reports where each silence starts and ends:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -af silencedetect=noise=-32dB:d=0.5 -f null -

noise is the silence threshold, and d is how many seconds a gap must last before it counts as silence. The output contains lines like these:

silence_start: 3.20
silence_end: 5.10 | silence_duration: 1.90

2. Work out what to keep

A Python script reads those lines and lists the sections between the silences. To keep the cuts from sounding abrupt, I leave a small margin at both ends of each section; 0.1 seconds is usually enough.

3. Cut and join

Once the ranges are known, one command can cut and join at the same time:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 \
  -vf "select='between(t,0,3.2)+between(t,5.1,12.4)',setpts=N/FRAME_RATE/TB" \
  -af "aselect='between(t,0,3.2)+between(t,5.1,12.4)',asetpts=N/SR/TB" \
  output.mp4

select keeps the same ranges in the picture that aselect keeps in the sound. setpts and asetpts renumber the timestamps; without them the video plays with gaps.

Tuning the threshold

SituationThresholdEffect
Starting point-32 dBWorks for most recordings
Word endings get clipped-40 dBOnly truly silent parts are cut
Pauses are left in-26 dBMore of the audio counts as silence

Recordings with a lot of background noise need a higher threshold, because even the moments when nobody speaks stay above -32 dB.

Exporting for Reels

  • 1080 × 1920 pixels at 30 frames per second.
  • -crf 20 -preset veryfast for a balance of quality and speed.
  • Loudness evened out to -14 LUFS with the loudnorm filter.

Why on the computer

The file never leaves the computer. There is no need to upload a video with people in it to a third-party service just to remove the pauses. It is faster, and easier on privacy.
