About

I study computer programming; at a dental clinic I look after IT and build automations with AI.

I work as an IT specialist at DCI Dental Clinic in Antalya. My job has two sides. The first is the clinic's day-to-day IT: I fix the problems that come up on the computers and other IT devices, and when one of the software systems misbehaves I'm the first to look at it.

The second is automation. Most of the clinic's patients come from abroad, so messages arrive at every hour and in many languages. To organise that traffic I build flows with n8n, Claude and Gemini.

At the same time I'm continuing my computer programming studies at Akdeniz University.
